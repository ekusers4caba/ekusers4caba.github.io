About the project

Inspired by old-school Halo fan-made custom lobbies, The hill game otherwise known as "Project Boulder" was created by Heather Brander and myself for the Game Engine Module of our 2nd year of University.

The main objective is to dodge incoming boulders as the player climbs the hill. The player is granted points for reaching the peak which is also multiplied by the time it took to get there.

At the start, players are given a total of three lives. Each boulder that hits the player results in the loss of one life. No lives results in the game ending.

This game was developed in the span of 12 weeks and was a great introduction to working with time restraints.

The hill game was given a pass grade upon submission.

Project Boulder has a few known bugs, like floating boulders when the player is teleported back down, which we plan on keeping as it is a great reminder of how far we've come and what we can achieve in such a short development cycle.
