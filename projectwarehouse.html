About the project

Locked Undead or originally named "Project Warehouse" is a showcase of our level design skills. Created for the Level Design module of the video games development course at UWS, this project was an introduction to the Unity engine and how different level designs can be implemented.

Together with Heather Brander and Stefan Dianocu we were able to showcase our creative abilities and technical skills to create an aesthetic environment.

With small interactions like a animated main menu, player movement and a toggleable lightswitch, players can explore the environment and immerse themselves in our work.

The assets within the project were all from the Unity Asset Store and audio files were sourced from Youtube and other online sources. This Project was made as a demonstration of our level design capabilities and not as a functional game.

Key features

  • Player movement and interactible animatedmain menu
  • An immersive demonstration of level design
  • aesthetic lighting, player torch and toggleable lightswitch
